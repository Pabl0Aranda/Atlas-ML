# Code of Conduct

Atlas ML exists to help people learn, and many of them are beginners. Everyone taking part in this project should feel welcome, whatever their background or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Treat every question as a fair question.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept constructive criticism, and be willing to admit mistakes.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

This code covers all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Pabl0Aranda). Reports are handled as privately as possible.

## What happens next

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a fair response. That can include a warning, removing content, or blocking someone from the project.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
